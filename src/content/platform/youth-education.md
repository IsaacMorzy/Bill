Our children deserve schools that prepare them for a future of opportunity. Bill Llia will fight for better learning facilities, well-trained teachers, and programs that keep young people engaged, employed, and empowered.

**School infrastructure:** Bill will prioritize rehabilitation of dilapidated classrooms, construction of new science laboratories, and provision of adequate sanitation facilities in every public school across the county.

**Scholarships and bursaries:** No child should be denied an education because of poverty. Bill will expand the county's scholarship and bursary programs, with transparent selection criteria that prioritize the most vulnerable students.

**Skills training:** We will establish vocational training centers in each constituency, offering courses in trades that have real market demand — from plumbing and electrical installation to digital skills and agribusiness.

**Sports and talent:** Bill believes that talent is evenly distributed but opportunity is not. He will invest in sports facilities, arts programs, and talent development initiatives that give young people pathways to success beyond the classroom.
