Every family in Nairobi County deserves quality healthcare close to home. As Governor, Bill Llia will make healthcare access the top priority of his administration.

**What we'll do:**

- Establish a fully equipped community health center in every ward, staffed by qualified nurses and clinical officers
- End drug stock-outs with a transparent supply chain system that ensures essential medicines are always available
- Expand maternal and child health programs to reduce preventable deaths
- Partner with the national government to bring health insurance enrollment drives directly to our communities

Bill has spent his career in healthcare. He knows that an ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure — and he will fight for a system that keeps Nairobi County healthy.
