Dr. Bill Llia has spent over a decade serving the communities of Nairobi County. He is not a career politician — he is a healthcare professional, a community organizer, and a father who believes that every family in Nairobi deserves access to quality healthcare, clean water, and real economic opportunity.

Bill began his career as a community health worker, visiting homes across the county's most underserved wards. He saw firsthand what happens when government fails to deliver: treatable illnesses become emergencies, talented young people remain unemployed, and small businesses struggle without basic infrastructure.

That experience shaped his belief that leadership is measured not by titles, but by results.

Today, Bill is running for Governor of Nairobi County because he knows that the status quo is not acceptable. He has a plan. He has the experience. And he has the integrity to lead with transparency and purpose.

When Bill says "Service with integrity. Leadership with heart," he means every word.
