I remember the day I first realized that good leadership could change everything.

I was working at a community health center in one of Nairobi County's underserved wards. A mother had walked six kilometers carrying her child, who was suffering from a severe fever. The health center had no malaria test kits — they had been out of stock for three weeks.

I made phone calls. I called the sub-county health officer, the nearby hospital, even a friend who worked at a pharmacy in town. Within four hours, we had the test kits. The child was diagnosed and treated, and the mother walked home the next day with medicine and hope.

That moment taught me something crucial: **our problems are solvable.** What we lack is not resources — it's leadership that cares enough to make things work.

## The reason I'm running

I'm running for Governor of Nairobi County because I've seen what happens when government shows up. I've seen health centers that run smoothly because someone competent is in charge. I've seen young people find work when given real skills training. I've seen communities thrive when they have clean water and good roads.

But I've also seen the opposite — and it breaks my heart.

Too many of our health centers are understaffed. Too many young people graduate with no path to employment. Too many families still lack access to clean water. These are not impossible problems. They are failures of leadership and priorities.

## My commitment to you

I am asking for your trust — not your vote. I will earn that trust by showing up, by listening, and by delivering measurable progress in the first 100 days of my administration.

This campaign is not about me. It is about us — the people of Nairobi County — coming together to demand the leadership we deserve.

Join us. The change starts now.

— Bill Llia
