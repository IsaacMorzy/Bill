Bill Llia today announced a bold new initiative that will provide skills training to 500 young people in Nairobi County during his first year as Governor.

The **Nairobi County Youth Skills and Enterprise Program** will offer training in high-demand trades including digital skills, agribusiness, renewable energy installation, and professional services. Each participant will receive:

- Three months of hands-on vocational training at a certified institution
- A paid apprenticeship placement with a local business or government department
- A mentorship pairing with an established professional in their field
- Access to a startup grant of up to KES 50,000 for graduates who start their own businesses

"We have thousands of talented young people in Nairobi County who are ready to work," Bill said at the announcement. "What they lack is not ability — it is opportunity. This program will close that gap."

## Partnership model

The program will be delivered through partnerships with technical and vocational education and training (TVET) institutions, local businesses, and community-based organizations. An advisory board comprising youth representatives, business leaders, and education experts will oversee the program's design and implementation.

"The worst thing we can do is train young people for jobs that don't exist," Bill added. "Every course in this program is designed based on real market demand. We are not teaching skills for the sake of it — we are building a pipeline to employment and entrepreneurship."
