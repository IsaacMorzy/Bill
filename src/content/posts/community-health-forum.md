On Saturday, April 1st, Bill Llia hosted a community health forum at the Embakasi Community Centre. Over 200 residents from across the constituency came together to discuss the state of healthcare in Nairobi County and share their ideas for improvement.

## What we heard

Residents raised several pressing concerns:

- **Drug stock-outs** remain a persistent problem at public health facilities, forcing patients to buy essential medicines at private pharmacies
- **Staffing shortages** mean long wait times at overcrowded facilities
- **Maternal health services** are inadequate, with some mothers delivering in facilities without emergency obstetric care
- **Health insurance coverage** remains low, with many families unable to afford NHIF premiums

## Bill's response

"I have seen these problems firsthand — in fact, I've lived them alongside you," Bill told the crowd. "As Governor, I will not just talk about fixing healthcare. I will put a community health center in every ward, ensure every facility is staffed and stocked, and work with the national government to bring health insurance to every family."

The forum was part of a series of community engagement events that Bill plans to hold in every constituency before the election.

All events are free and open to the public.
