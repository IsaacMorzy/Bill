NAIROBI — Dr. Bill Llia, a community health advocate with over 12 years of service in Nairobi County, today officially launched his campaign for Governor at a rally attended by more than 1,000 supporters at the Nairobi Arena.

In his announcement speech, Llia outlined a comprehensive platform centered on four pillars: healthcare access, education and youth development, economic empowerment, and water and sanitation infrastructure.

"Nairobi County has tremendous potential, but too many of our people are being left behind," Llia told the crowd. "I am running because I believe that every family in this county deserves access to quality healthcare, clean water, and a real opportunity to build a better life."

Llia's campaign has already gained support from community organizations, religious leaders, and youth groups across the county's constituencies. The campaign is expected to focus on door-to-door outreach, community forums, and a robust digital organizing effort.

"We are building a movement of ordinary citizens who are tired of empty promises and ready for real change," said campaign manager Jane Akinyi. "Bill Llia has the experience, the integrity, and the vision to lead this county forward."
